Privacy Policy

channel-uploader is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload and manage videos on the owner's own YouTube channels.

  • It uses the YouTube Data API solely for the owner's own Google account.
  • It does not collect, store, or share anyone else's personal data.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's computer and are never sent elsewhere.
  • Access can be revoked at any time in Google Account → Security → Third-party apps & services.
